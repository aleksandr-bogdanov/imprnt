# Mission

Design tools that respect the people who use them, stay independent enough to choose the work, and
keep health and relationships ahead of any single project.

## Operating rules
- Default to one main client at a time. Depth over a crowded roster.
- Protect sleep first. A bad week of sleep costs more than any deadline saves.
- Money buys runway, and runway buys the freedom to say no.
