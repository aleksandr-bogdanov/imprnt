**Folders** are for you to browse, never for search to use. `recall` ignores them, so where you put a note never affects whether you find it. That frees the folders to be an honest map of your life instead of a filing system you fight.

## The layout

Three kinds of folder, each with a real reason to exist. Entities get one home and everything links to them, domains hold most of your content and you pick which ones, forms are split out by how you use them.

## What carries the meaning

Three labels do the real work, and keeping them separate is what stops the folders from sprawling.

<MeaningTable />

The meaning rides in the header, not the path. A note in a life-area folder also says `domain:` in that header, and `check` complains if the folder and the field disagree. Move the note or export it, and its **meaning** comes along.

## Tags grow on their own

`_tags.md` holds your tags and a synonym map, and the list grows with **no approval step**.
