**Folders** are for you to browse, never for search to use. `recall` ignores them entirely. One carve-out: folders starting with `_` or `.` hold control files, and both search and `check` skip them, so don't put notes there. That frees the folders to be an honest map of your life.

## What carries the meaning

The meaning rides in a few short labels at the top of every note, so a note keeps what it means even when it moves.

<VaultLayoutAnatomy />

A note in a life-area folder also names that area in the header (`domain:`), and `check` complains when the folder and the field disagree. That covers the shipped set and any area you add yourself, once you name it under `## Domains` in `vault/_folders.md`.

## A folder you share

Some things belong to more than one person: the flat, the bills, the dates you both need. A **mount** is a folder inside `vault/` holding a tree kept somewhere else, usually a small shared repo two people check out at the same path. Name it under `## Mounts` in `vault/_folders.md` and `check` stops asking its notes to link into your graph, because their graph lives on the other side. A mount can carry its own `_folders.md`, so its folders follow its rules, and its snapshots sit in `<mount>/_raw/`.

Sharing a note is moving it. `imprnt vault move life/flat household/life` puts the note in the shared folder, deletes the private copy, rewrites every link to it, and prints who can read it now. There is no copy verb, on purpose: two copies of one fact drift apart. The move refuses a note whose `source:` points into your private `raw/`, or whose links to people or organizations have no answer inside the mount. Afterwards `check` reports `seam-leak` for a shared note whose link resolves only in your vault, and `seam-dead-source` for one still pointing at your private snapshots.
